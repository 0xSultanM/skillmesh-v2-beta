Privacy

Your career. Your data.

A clear look at what happens to your information.

Private by design. Not just a setting.

SkillMesh is built so that we never need to keep your data.

01

What happens to your resume

Your file is read in memory on our server to extract its text, then discarded. It is never written to disk or to a database.

If AI extraction is enabled, text is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic). We remove email addresses, phone numbers, street addresses and social profile links first.

We don't log request contents.

02

Job postings

Job URLs are fetched once to read the posting. We only read public pages, never pages that need a login.

Screenshots are read in your browser using on-device OCR. The image is never uploaded.

03

Your history

Saved analyses and roadmap progress live only in this browser, in local storage.

Delete them at any time using "Delete all local data" in History.

04

What we do store

Only public data: our skill taxonomy and a cache of the free learning resources we recommend—tutorials, playlists and documentation.
